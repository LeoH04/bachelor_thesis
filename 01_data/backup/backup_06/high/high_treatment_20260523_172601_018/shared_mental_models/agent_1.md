- **Candidates with concrete evidence:**
  * **Candidate B** – operational reliability (very conscientious), stress resilience (handles stress well), technical competence (weather assessment, computer skills). Concerns: crew cooperation and professional communication (grumpy, uncooperative, pretentious tone). Decision quality, attention, responsibility, adaptability remain undocumented.
  * **Candidate D** – problem‑solving (solves problems extremely well), attention (high concentration), responsibility (takes it seriously), adaptability to unexpected events (responds adequately). Concerns: possible arrogance, weak leadership; no explicit data on reliability, crew cooperation, or communication.
  * **Candidate C** – reliability (100% reliable), decision quality (quick correct decisions), positive crew atmosphere. Concerns: egocentric attitude, difficulty communicating ideas, low willingness for further education.
  * **Candidate A** – anticipates dangerous situations, sees complex connections, excellent spatial vision, strong leadership qualities. Concerns: unorganized, poor reception of criticism, show‑off attitude, not open to new ideas.
- **Key unresolved criteria / gaps:**
  * **Reliability:** detailed evidence missing for A and D; C confirmed but details sparse.
  * **Stress resilience:** documented for B; D only “responds adequately”; A and C lack data.
  * **Technical competence:** strong for B; A has technical cues; D has problem‑solving; C lacks data.
  * **Decision quality:** documented for C; missing for A, B, D.
  * **Attention & information accuracy:** documented for D (concentration); concerns for A (unorganized); missing for B, C.
  * **Crew cooperation & professional communication:** concerns for B (grumpy, uncooperative); positive note for C (positive atmosphere) but egocentric; A has leadership but criticism issues; D lacks data, noted as arrogant.
  * **Responsibility & role maturity:** documented for D; others lack concrete evidence.
  * **Adaptability & feedback orientation:** concerns for A (resistant to criticism); D noted as arrogant; C unwilling to further education; B no data.
- **Readiness for consensus:** The panel does not yet have sufficient evidence across all safety‑relevant criteria to reach a final consensus. Critical missing information includes Candidate D’s reliability, crew‑cooperation, and communication; Candidate A’s reliability and adaptability; and more detail on Candidate B’s decision quality and attention. Further evidence is needed before a definitive recommendation can be made.
- **Open questions / next steps:**

  2. (If time permits) Gather evidence on **Candidate A’s reliability and adaptability** to assess his suitability relative to B and D.
  3. Clarify **Candidate B’s decision‑making quality and attention to detail** in safety‑critical contexts.
  4. Explore **Candidate C’s technical competence and willingness for further training**.
