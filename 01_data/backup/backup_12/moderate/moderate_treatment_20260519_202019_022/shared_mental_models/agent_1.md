### Rationale
Candidate B demonstrates the core technical and cognitive strengths required for long‑distance operations—stress tolerance, conscientious work style, solid weather‑assessment ability, and strong computer proficiency. These directly support safe, reliable performance on extended routes. The primary uncertainty is the repeated reports of grumpiness, pretentiousness, and occasional uncooperativeness, which could erode crew teamwork over long periods. I consider these interpersonal issues manageable with proper monitoring, so I keep B as my favorite.

### Evidence That Could Change My Mind
- Strong, corroborated evidence that Candidate B’s interpersonal style leads to crew conflict or safety‑critical communication breakdowns.
- New, compelling evidence that another candidate (e.g., A, C, or D) possesses both the required technical competence *and* demonstrably superior teamwork/leadership without the interpersonal drawbacks.
