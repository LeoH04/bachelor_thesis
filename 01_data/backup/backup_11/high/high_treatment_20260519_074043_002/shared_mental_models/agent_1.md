## Emerging Group View
<!-- SMM_SECTION:emerging_group_view:start -->

- **Agreements:** All agents agree that technical competence, stress resilience, and reliable decision‑making are essential for long‑distance flights.
- **Disagreements / Tensions:**
  * Candidate B’s interpersonal style (pretentious, grumpy, tone issues) vs. Candidate C’s crew‑focused attitude but communication difficulty.
  * Candidate A’s strong anticipatory and leadership abilities are offset by health, unfriendly, and organizational concerns.
  * Candidate D’s excellent problem‑solving and responsibility are countered by arrogance, weak leadership, moodiness, and loner tendencies.
- **Uncertainties:**
  * Severity and safety impact of B’s communication/attitude issues.
  * Whether C’s communication deficits materially affect safety under workload.
  * Impact of A’s unhealthy habits and D’s moodiness on reliability during ultra‑long routes.
