## Emerging Group View
<!-- SMM_SECTION:emerging_group_view:start -->
- No consensus: two agents (1 & 3) favor Candidate B, while agent 2 favors Candidate A.
- Key disagreement points:
  - **Candidate B**: Repeated concerns about pretentious, grumpy, and occasionally uncooperative communication style that could hinder crew teamwork on long‑distance flights.
  - **Candidate A**: Reputation as a show‑off and resistance to new ideas, potentially affecting teamwork and long‑term professional growth.
  - **Candidate C** and **Candidate D**: Both have solid technical/problem‑solving strengths but are not favored due to communication/teamwork weaknesses (C’s egocentricity and poor communication; D’s moodiness and loner tendency).
- The group remains blocked by whether the communication/personality weaknesses of the leading candidates can be mitigated through training versus the safety‑critical need for seamless crew interaction on long‑distance operations.
